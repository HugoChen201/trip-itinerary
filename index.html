✨ Muse's recommendations

Picks timed to your dates — late November in Seoul, early December in Tokyo.

🇰🇷 Seoul · Nov 23–28
🎭
2026 Seoul Autumn FestaSep 18 – Nov 29

A citywide performing-arts festival — dance, theater, music and traditional arts across Seoul under the theme "Seoul's Autumn: The City Becomes Art." It overlaps your entire Seoul stay. Details ↗

🍂
Late autumn foliage

You'll catch the tail end of fall colors — Namsan Park, Seoul Forest and the palace grounds (Gyeongbokgung, Changdeokgung) are the classic spots.

🍢
Eat: Gwangjang Market & Myeongdong

Gwangjang for bindaetteok and knife-cut noodles, Myeongdong for street-food grazing after dark. Cold-weather food hits different.

💡
Just missed: Seoul Winter Festival

The big winter light festival (Hangang River, Gwanghwamun, DDP) starts Dec 4 — a few days after you leave for Tokyo. Save it for a future trip.

🇯🇵 Tokyo · Nov 28 – Dec 5
🍁
Rikugien Garden night illuminationsNov 25 – Dec 6

One of Tokyo's most beautiful Edo-period landscape gardens, open 6–8:30 PM for illuminated autumn foliage — right in the middle of your Tokyo days, easy JR Yamanote access. Details ↗

✨
Winter illuminations

Peak season while you're there: Roppongi Hills (Nov 4–Dec 25), Roppongi Midtown (early Nov–Dec 25), Shibuya Blue Cave (early Dec–Dec 25) and Marunouchi near Tokyo Station. Guide ↗

🎄
Christmas markets

Meiji Jingu Gaien market runs from Nov 21, and Yokohama Red Brick Warehouse from Nov 21 — glühwein and a good evening out. December guide ↗

⛩️
Day trip: Kamakura + Enoshima

Temples and shrines in Kamakura by day, Enoshima's seaside illuminations after dark — about an hour from central Tokyo.
