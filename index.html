🇰🇷 Seoul · Nov 23–27
🎭
2026 Seoul Autumn FestaSep 19 – Nov 29

A citywide performing-arts festival — dance, theater, music and traditional arts across Seoul under the theme "Seoul's Autumn: The City Becomes Art." It overlaps your entire Seoul stay. Details →

🍂
Late autumn foliage

You'll catch the tail end of fall colors — Namsan Park, Seoul Forest and the palace grounds (Gyeongbokgung, Changdeokgung) are the classic spots. Details →

🍢
Eat: Gwangjang Market

Gwangjang for bindaetteok and knife-cut noodles — travelers call it the single best food stop in Seoul, especially in November when the stall steam hits the crisp air. Details →

🧺
Mangwon Market (the locals' Gwangjang)

The locals' answer to Gwangjang — "fresh produce + authentic eats, way less touristy." Tteokbokki and fried-chicken stalls are the standouts, plus vintage shops on the side streets. Details →

🍜
Myeongdong Kyoja (kalguksu)

A repeatedly-named restaurant: handmade kalguksu (knife-cut noodles) and mandu (dumplings), ~₩11,000. "A must-visit for food lovers" — perfect cold-weather lunch. Details →

🍲
Tosokchon Samgyetang

The famous ginseng chicken soup spot — whole chicken stuffed with rice in hot broth, ~₩15,000–20,000. Five minutes from Gyeongbokgung: the classic post-palace lunch. Details →

🍗
Chimaek: fried chicken + beer

"Korean fried chicken reaches its seasonal peak during November nights" — a social ritual, not just a meal. Hongdae has the highest density of chicken-and-beer joints. Details →

🦀
Noryangjin Fish Market

"You pick your seafood downstairs, restaurants upstairs cook it for you" — Seoul's most hands-on food experience. Sashimi, crab, live octopus (sannakji) for the brave. Details →

🌃
Myeongdong street food at night

"Shopping + street food heaven (come hungry!)" — the TikTok-consensus first-timer stop, best after dark when the stalls light up. Details →

🏯
Gyeongbokgung Palace + hanbok rental

The #1 first-timer cultural stop. Renting a hanbok gets you FREE palace entry (~₩15,000–30,000 for 4 hours from shops around the palace). Details →

🍁
Changdeokgung Secret Garden

"My favorite thing in Seoul — I did it in every season." The guided Secret Garden tour is the standout; regulars say the tour (not just the grounds) is what makes it. Details →

🏘️
Bukchon Hanok Village (go early)

Traditional hanok streets — gorgeous but packed by midday. "Go early morning to beat the crowds," then fold in Insadong's tea houses. Details →

⛰️
Eunpyeong Hanok Village

"Traditional hanok houses framed by mountains, but with none of the Bukchon crowds." The quieter alternative, with Bukhansan as the backdrop. Details →

☕
Ikseon-dong + Seosulla-gil

Hanok alleyways converted into cafes, boutiques, and bars — "getting lost in Ikseon-dong's web of lanes is part of the fun." Named one of 2026's coolest neighborhoods. Details →

🎶
Hongdae after dark

"The heart of Seoul's nightlife with street performers, quirky shops, bars, photobooths + all the vibes." By day: indie shops and the Saturday Free Market; by night: buskers, clubs, bars. Details →

🏭
Seongsu-dong

Seoul's current "it" neighborhood — converted warehouses, Gentle Monster's surreal Haus Nowhere flagship (giant sleeping dog), pop-ups, K-beauty. "Way better vibes" than Myeongdong, per TikTok. Details →

💧
Cheonggyecheon Stream evening walk

"A peaceful riverside walk right in the city center, perfect for a calm reset" — best at night when it's lit up. Details →

🗼
N Seoul Tower (Namsan)

Cable car or hike up Namsan for the classic Seoul panorama — go at sunset and stay for the city lights. Details →

🌳
Seoul Forest

"One of my favorite parks in Seoul, especially scenic during autumn" — metasequoia and ginkgo avenues, free deer feeding, butterfly garden. Details →

🌅
Nodeul Island sunset

The insider Han River pick: "Don't waste time at the crowded Han parks — go to Nodeul Island instead." Mostly locals, great sunset views, live-music venue on the island. Details →

🌉
Hangang (Han River) parks

Yeouido or Banpo Hangang Park for riverside walks and night views — grab convenience-store snacks and join the locals by the water. Details →

🍞
Gyeongui Line Forest Park + salt bread

A trendy linear park built on an old rail line, lined with cafes. The TikTok-famous stop: Jayeondo Sogeumppang — "don't miss the famous salt bread." Details →

♨️
Jjimjilbang (Korean sauna)

"Like Japanese onsen on steroids" — the #1 weird-in-a-good-way Seoul experience. Aquafield (inside Starfield malls) is the premium pick; Hongdae's 24-hour Bulgama Sauna is the budget one. Details →

🎤
Coin noraebang (karaoke)

Private karaoke rooms by the hour — the classic 3-guys night activity. Coin noraebang booths are cheapest (~₩1,000–4,000/song). Open late. Details →

👕
Henley hunt: where to shop

Cheapest oversized Henleys (~₩10–20k): Goto Mall (Express Bus Terminal underground — the go-to budget spot) and Myeongdong Underground Shopping (by the hotel, good for Day 1). Trendier picks: Hongdae Brand Market and Garosugil. Size up to XL–2XL — KR sizing runs small.

🛍️
Dongdaemun + DDP (+ Namdaemun)

Seoul's late-night shopping district — malls open past midnight — wrapped around Zaha Hadid's alien-looking Dongdaemun Design Plaza. Good Thursday-night shopping run. Details →

🇰🇷 Incheon · Nov 27 — airport day
🏮
Incheon Chinatown

Korea's oldest Chinatown — and the birthplace of jajangmyeon. Compact, walkable, and a good lunch stop on your way to ICN. Details →

🧚
Songwol-dong Fairytale Village

A 19th-century foreign-settler village restored with whimsical fairy-tale murals and sculptures — "quirky but cute," great on a sunny day. A Goblin filming location is nearby. Details →

🍗
Sinpo International Market

Traditional market famous nationwide for dakgangjeong (sweet-crunchy fried chicken) — "famous spice coated fried chicken for 17000 won/bucket worth the trip." Details →

🏛️
Jajangmyeon Museum

Housed in Gonghwachun, the first restaurant to serve jajangmyeon in Korea — a fun, quick cultural stop for food nerds right before eating the real thing. Details →

⚓
Incheon Open Port Area

The most historically rich part of Incheon — 1883 open-port-era buildings in Chinese, Japanese, and Western styles. The Jemulpo Club (1901, free) and mural streets are the highlights. Details →

🌳
Jayu Park (Freedom Park)

Korea's first Western-style park, with the Seokjeongnu Pavilion viewpoint over Incheon's coast and port — a calm sunset/stroll spot to end the old-town loop. Details →

🛶
Songdo Central Park

The centerpiece of Korea's futuristic planned city — kayak the seawater canal, stroll the gardens, see the skyline. About 30 min from the airport. Details →

🔭
G-Tower Observatory

Free 33rd-floor observatory with panoramic views of Songdo, Incheon Bridge, and the West Sea — go at sunset for golden light on the water. Details →

🎡
Wolmido boardwalk

Retro seaside boardwalk with the famous Viking ride and Disco Pang Pang (bumper cars to club music, famous from Korean TV) — charmingly dated, fun for an hour before ICN. Details →

🚲
Yeongjong Seaside Rail Bike

A 30–45-min pedal rail-bike ride along the coast on the same island as ICN airport — the ideal "last activity before the airport" stop. Details →

🇯🇵 Tokyo · Nov 27 – Dec 5
🍁
Rikugien Garden night illuminationsNov 25 – Dec 6

One of Tokyo's most beautiful Edo-period landscape gardens, open 6–8:30 PM for illuminated autumn foliage — right in the middle of your Tokyo days, easy JR Yamanote access. Details →

💡
Roppongi Hills Keyakizaka Illumination~Nov 4 – Dec 25

The iconic Tokyo winter shot: 400m of "Snow & Blue" LEDs down Keyakizaka — stand at the bridge near Louis Vuitton for Tokyo Tower centered in frame. Details →

🥂
Marunouchi Naka-dori Illumination~Nov 13 – Feb 15

340+ trees in "Champagne Gold" along the avenue by Tokyo Station — feels like a European boulevard; less flashy, more elegant than Roppongi. Details →

🔵
Shibuya Blue Cave (Ao no Dokutsu)~Dec 4 – 25

A dense tunnel of blue LEDs from Shibuya toward Yoyogi Park — "incredibly famous... surreal, immersive blue world." Starts Dec 4, DURING your trip. Details →

⛸️
Tokyo Midtown Starlight Garden + ice rink~Nov 13 – Dec 25

A light show with music, not just decoration — and an outdoor skating rink under the lights, the festive pick for a group. Details →

🎄
Azabudai Hills Christmas Market~Nov 22 – Dec 25

Free-entry Christmas market + illuminations at the base of Japan's tallest skyscraper — modern and less tourist-trodden than the big ones. Details →

🎢
Yomiuriland Jewellumination~Oct 23 – Apr 5

6.5 million gemstone-themed lights by world-famous lighting designer Mikiko Ishii — ride a rollercoaster THROUGH the lights or view from the Ferris wheel. Details →

🍷
Tokyo Christmas Market @ Shin-ToyosuNov 20 – Dec 25

The big Tokyo Christmas Market moved to Shin-Toyosu this year (¥1,000–2,000 entry) — glühwein and a good evening out. Details →

🗼
Christmas Market @ Shiba ParkDec 4 – 25

A second Christmas market run at Shiba Park by Tokyo Tower — pairs perfectly with the tower lit up at night. Details →

🎅
Christmas Advent in Ueno ParkNov 19 – Dec 25

Debuting in Ueno Park — free entry, 150 Santas from around the world. The budget-friendly Christmas stop. Details →

🧱
Yokohama Red Brick Christmas Marketfrom Nov 21

The classic: Red Brick Warehouse Christmas market, Minato Mirai lights and Japan's biggest Chinatown — 30 minutes by train, easy evening trip. Details →

⛪
Meiji Jingu Gaien Christmas Marketfrom Nov 21

The Gaien market runs from Nov 21 — glühwein under the ginkgo trees, two seasonal hits in one spot. Details →

🌇
Shibuya Sky

360° open-air rooftop 229m above the Scramble — "widely considered the best sunset spot," "the one most first-timers end up loving most." Details →

🗼
Tokyo Tower (Main Deck)

The budget classic: ~¥1,200–1,500, no advance booking, the classic look — best at night when lit. Details →

🏙️
Tokyo Metro Gov Building (FREE observatory)

FREE observatory in Shinjuku — plus free evening projection-mapping shows on the building (7 PM, every 30 min till 9:30 PM). Best free night activity in the city. Details →

🍂
Shinjuku Gyoen

Golden ginkgos + last maples in early December. "There were the last vestiges of maple leaves and golden ginkgos. It was absolutely lovely." Details →

🌟
Meiji Jingu Gaien ginkgo avenuepeak late Nov

Rows of golden ginkgo trees — peak color lands exactly on your Tokyo dates. Go on a weekday morning to dodge the crowds. Details →

🥾
Mt. Takao (day trip)

Easy hiking + cable car option, autumn leaves festival — TikTok/Reddit's go-to "nature escape" from the city, ~50 min away. Details →

⛩️
Asakusa / Senso-ji (early morning)

Tokyo's oldest temple — "especially in the morning before crowds build." Nakamise street snacks after. Details →

🐱
Yanaka Ginza

"The Tokyo that survived": pre-war wooden houses, 70-shop traditional shotengai, known as Tokyo's "cat town." Try menchi katsu at Niku no Sato. Details →

👕
Shimokitazawa

Vintage shops (Flamingo, Pug Tokyo), indie theaters, tiny live-music venues (Basement Bar, Shelter). Artsy/bohemian, great for an afternoon browse. Details →

🎸
Koenji

Shimokitazawa's rougher, cheaper sibling: vintage + live music, strong local-creative vibe, fewer tourists. Details →

🦢
Kichijoji + Inokashira Park

Repeatedly voted Tokyo's most livable neighborhood — relaxed trendy shops, park boating, "more local and laid-back vibe." Details →

🏮
Ueno / Ameyoko

Loud, bustling open-air market street: wholesale shops, cheap eats, people-watching. Good budget shopping stop. Details →

🍣
Tsukiji Outer Market (early morning)

Seafood breakfast (tuna sashimi, grilled eel/scallops) — but go before 9 AM. At lunch it's "absolutely jam packed... eating food in a busy street market is very stressful." Details →

🍱
Depachika (dept-store food halls)

"One of the best discoveries of our trip... ready-to-eat food of every variety — bento, katsu, sushi" (Isetan Shinjuku etc.). Cheap, incredible quality, great for group grazing. Details →

🍜
Ichiran Ramen

Foreigner-friendly ticket-machine solo ramen — "the ordering process is very efficient... I can see why it's popular with tourists." ~¥1,000/bowl. Details →

🥘
Monjayaki on Monja Street, Tsukishima

Tokyo's local specialty — a runny savory pancake you cook yourself on the grill. Fun group activity; popular spots line up at lunch. Details →

🍶
Omoide Yokocho + Golden Gai

Yakitori smoke alleys and six tiny streets of closet-sized bars — the quintessential Tokyo night out. Details →

🤼
Sumo morning practice (asageiko)

No Tokyo tournament during your dates (Nov basho is in Fukuoka) — but stables hold morning practice, and a Viator listing shows bookable Dec 1–30, 2026 Mon–Sat 8–10 AM slots. Details →

🎤
Karaoke Kan (private-room karaoke)

The classic group night — "many locations offer late-night deals." Perfect for 3 guys. Details →

🕹️
Arcades: Taito Station / Round1 / Super Potato

Claw machines, rhythm games; Round1 adds bowling + karaoke under one roof (some 24h); Super Potato for retro games. Akihabara/Shinjuku. Details →

🏎️
Street go-karting

"One of the more wacky (and popular) tourist activities" — kart through Shibuya/Shinjuku/Akihabara in costume. ¥7,200–18,000. Details →

♨️
Toyosu Senkyaku Banrai + Manyo Club onsen

Edo-village food complex next to Toyosu Market plus a 24-hour onsen (~¥4,000): indoor/outdoor baths, rooftop 360° foot bath (free), relaxation rooms, buffet. TikTok: "Worth the money for sure!" Details →

⛩️
Day trip: Kamakura + Enoshima

Temples and shrines in Kamakura by day, Enoshima's seaside illuminations after dark — about an hour from central Tokyo. Details →

🌉
Enoshima "Jewels of Shonan"late Nov – Feb

The whole island of Enoshima lights up for winter — pair it with the Kamakura day trip and stay for the night illuminations at Samuel Cocking Garden. Details →

🗻
Day trip: Hakone

Clear winter air = best Mt. Fuji visibility of the year. Open-air museum, Lake Ashi, onsen — about 2 hours via the Odakyu Romancecar from Shinjuku. Details →

🏯
Day trip: Nikko

UNESCO Toshogu Shrine dusted with early winter; Kegon Falls partially freezes. "Wish I'd given Nikko at least another day." Details →

🗻
Day trip: Kawaguchiko / Mt. Fuji

Winter has the year's clearest Fuji views — Chureito Pagoda (arrive before 7 AM to beat crowds), hoto noodle lunch, lakeside onsen. Go on a weekday. Details →

✨
Ashikaga Flower Park "Garden of Light"~Late Oct – early Feb

5M+ LEDs on the famous wisteria trellises — "voted one of the best illumination events in the world," Reddit calls it "jaw-dropping." ~¥1,300. Details →

🏙️
Half day: Yokohama

Red Brick Warehouse Christmas market, Minato Mirai lights and Japan's biggest Chinatown — 30 minutes by train, easy evening trip. Details →

💠
teamLab

Planets or Borderless — the immersive digital-art museums. Tickets sell out days ahead, so book online before you fly. Details →
